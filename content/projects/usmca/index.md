The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) substituted the North America Free
Trade Agreement (NAFTA) in 2020, updating trade rules for one of the world’s largest free trade
zones. Some key changes include stricter rules of origin and higher wage requirements in the
automotive industry, as well as a requirement for broader worker representation in collective bargaining
in Mexico. This project studies what is the impact of the new provisions on employment
and wages in Mexico, using administrative employer-employee matched data.
